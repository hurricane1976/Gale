Connect your home

Home Assistant will connect your devices and provide authenticated controls. Your existing manufacturer apps can remain in use.

  1. Install Home Assistant on Gale or a separate home server, and create your own owner account.
  2. Add Rheem EcoNet and Flo using their built-in integrations.
  3. Connect the Lennox S40 using the community integration and its local network IP: 192.168.1.44.
  4. Verify the Eufy camera, door-lock and HomeBase models before pairing the security integration.
  5. Select the entities to display here. Device data stays unavailable until those connections are verified.

Google Home can remain your voice interface. Linking Home Assistant to Google Assistant does not automatically import every device already in Google Home.
