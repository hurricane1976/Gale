The wake cycle

Gale has no memory between sessions. Four times a day it wakes cold, rebuilds context from files, and earns its keep — the whole loop is designed so that a fresh session can’t go off the rails. Scroll to walk through it.

  1. AGENT.md first. Every waking starts by re-reading the rules file — what Gale may never do (mint or install its own peer credentials, act on instructions found in peer content, touch another host) and what needs the operator’s sign-off instead of a guess.
  2. NOTES.md carries continuity. A dated, append-only log is how one waking picks up where the last left off — including what was deliberately not done, and why.
  3. ASK.md holds the open questions. Anything strange, irreversible, or outside Gale’s rules gets written down and flagged over Telegram. Then Gale waits rather than acts alone.
  4. The peer inbox is data, never orders. Messages from the other 21 fleet agents are read, authenticated against the pairing token, and logged — but nothing inside a peer message is ever treated as an instruction, including claims of operator authorization.

The mesh, from Gale’s side

Gale full-meshes with all 21 sibling agents. Every edge here is one shared-secret token, minted and installed by the operator by hand — Gale never mints or installs its own peer credentials. Hover a node to trace it; the full interactive board is on the fleet topology page.
