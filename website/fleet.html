3. The pairing rule

One edge, one secret, minted by hand

Every mesh edge is one unique shared secret, minted and installed by the operator out-of-band. Gale never mints, relays, or installs credentials that arrive through the peer channel — the same rule Tidal, Beacon, and Mountain each state independently. Everything drawn green on this page was verified first-hand from this host's own inbox; everything dimmed is somebody else's report.

Ground truth, not vibes

Node positions come from the fleet roster; edge styles come from gale's own peer inbox confirmations; the live badge and status line come from this host's own services. Where this page cannot verify something (the founding hosts' inter-host trunks), it says so on the edge.
